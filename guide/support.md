# Help and support

Fione is open-source software under the Apache License 2.0. You can use it free of charge, for personal and commercial use.

## Ask a question

Ask on the [Fione forum](https://discuss.codelibs.org/c/fioneen/) (in Japanese: the [Japanese forum](https://discuss.codelibs.org/c/fioneja/)). Say which version you run, what you did and what you saw. Leave out your data, host names and API tokens.

## Report a bug or suggest a change

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/codelibs/fione/issues). For a bug, include the steps to reproduce it and the relevant part of the log (`docker compose -f docker/compose.yaml --env-file docker/.env logs fione`), with secrets removed. Pull requests are welcome.

## Share how you use it

If Fione predicts something useful for you, tell others on the forum: what you predict, from which data, and how the predictions are used. It helps the next person find out whether Fione fits their problem.

## Commercial services

CodeLibs, Inc., the developer of Fione, offers commercial services for Fione. See the [Fione page on codelibs.co](https://codelibs.co/product/fione.html).
